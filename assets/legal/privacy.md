# Onshore 3Rivers AI — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 2026-09-07_

> The authoritative version is at https://3rivers.onshoretech.ai/privacy.

## Who this app is for

Onshore 3Rivers AI is an internal operations tool for 3Rivers staff. You
sign in with an account issued by your organization. It is not a
consumer app and is not intended for the general public.

## What the app collects

- **Account data:** your name, email/username, and role, from your
  organization's identity provider (Keycloak); sign-in records (time,
  device type).
- **Operational data you view or act on:** work orders, shipments,
  supplier and vendor records, message history, and video check-in
  sessions — all owned by your organization.
- **Diagnostics:** crash and error logs, which may include your user ID
  and the screen where an error occurred.

The app does **not** collect your location, contacts, photos, or
advertising identifiers, and contains **no advertising or third-party
tracking SDKs**.

## How it's used

Only to provide the staff portal: authenticating you, showing your
organization's operational records, and starting video check-ins. Your
data is not sold and is not used for advertising.

## Storage and security

Data is transmitted over TLS and stored on Onshore-operated servers in
the United States. Access is limited to your organization and to Onshore
personnel who need it to run or support the service.

## Retention

Operational records are retained by your organization per its own
policy. Diagnostic logs are kept up to 90 days.

## Your choices

- **Access / correction:** contact your 3Rivers administrator or
  support@onshoretech.ai.
- **Deletion:** request it from Settings → Delete account. Operational
  records remain in your organization's system of record.
